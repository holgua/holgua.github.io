§5.2 — YoY Trends

  • Intelligent Cloud: Revenue +30% to $34.7B; Azure +40%. IC operating margin compressed 1.8 ppt (39.7%) due to AI infrastructure cost of revenue +47%.
  • P&BP: Revenue +17% to $35.0B; M365 Commercial cloud +19%. Operating margin expanded 1.9 ppt to 59.9% — efficiency gains in M365 cloud more than offset AI cost investment.
  • More Personal Computing: Revenue −1% to $13.2B; Gaming −7%; Search advertising +9%. Operating margin improved 1.4 ppt to 27.8%.
  • Gross margin: Total 67.6% (−1.1 ppt); Microsoft Cloud 66% (−4 ppt). AI infrastructure cost absorption is the compressor; efficiency gains in Azure and M365 Commercial cloud partially offset.
  • Operating leverage: Operating income +20% on revenue +18% — software scaling working. Operating expenses grew only 9%.
