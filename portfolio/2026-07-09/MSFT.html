About Microsoft

Microsoft makes its money in three ways: subscriptions to Microsoft 365 and Dynamics 365 (productivity and business-management software sold seat-by-seat to companies); computing capacity on demand through Azure (its cloud platform, used increasingly for artificial-intelligence workloads); and Windows licences, gaming, and search advertising through Bing. Revenue for the fiscal year ending June 2025 was $281.7 billion; operating income was $128.5 billion — a 45.6% operating margin that ranks among the highest sustained in enterprise software.

The common thread is lock-in. Azure Active Directory (now Entra ID) is the enterprise identity system that governs which employees can access which software. Replacing Microsoft means replacing email, security monitoring, device management, business software, and collaboration simultaneously — an architectural dependency, not a habitual one. That is why enterprise customers rarely leave and why the business generates pricing power and recurring revenue that are structurally hard to erode.

What Could Still Go Wrong

  • Azure decelerates below 25%. The single most important metric. Falling to mid-teens growth while capital expenditure remains elevated fires exit condition one — the core compounding thesis breaks.
  • Free-cash-flow conversion does not recover. If the ratio remains below 0.65 through two full fiscal years without Azure AI improvement, exit condition two fires. The next full-year result (expected July 2026) is the checkpoint.
  • IRS dispute escalates. A Tax Court referral or single-quarter tax charge above $5 billion triggers exit condition four. The assessed amount ($28.9 billion plus penalties) is large; settlement at the upper bound would not threaten solvency but would impair intrinsic value by several dollars per share.
  • FTC structural action. A formal complaint seeking divestiture of Teams or unbundling of cloud and AI services would erode the switching-cost moat at the thesis's centre of gravity.
  • OpenAI losses scale. A $4.7 billion equity-method charge was recognised in fiscal year 2025. If the OpenAI relationship keeps generating charges without offsetting product benefit, shareholder value transfers to a privately held partner.

The Rules

Six branches govern this position:

If the thesis is intact (all exit conditions unbroken, as today): hold. No additional position at current prices — margin of safety is insufficient for a new entry.

If the thesis weakens (a driver deteriorates without an exit condition firing): do not add. Monitor for one more quarter, determine whether the weakness is cyclical or structural, and escalate to the broken-thesis review if a second consecutive quarter confirms the signal.

If the thesis breaks (any single exit condition fires): exit the full position within 30 days. No averaging down.

If the price falls below the buy band (earnings yield above 5.0%, approximately $160): confirm no exit condition has triggered, then add in tranches. First tranche below $352 (40% margin of safety from the base-case fair value); second tranche below $322 (40% from the earnings-power estimate). Each tranche is 25–33% of the intended add-on allocation.

If the price rises above the trim band (earnings yield below 2.0%, approximately $401): trim 20–25% of the position to harvest the premium. Do not trim below 50% of the holding unless a thesis exit condition also fires.

If the price rises above the exit band (earnings yield below 1.5%, approximately $1,605): exit the full position. At that level the yield is less than half the risk-free rate and no margin of safety remains. Redeploy at the next cheap-edge crossing. No re-entry within 90 days.
