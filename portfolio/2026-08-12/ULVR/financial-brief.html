PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Unilever PLC is a global FMCG company with EUR 50.5bn in FY2025 annual revenue across Beauty & Wellbeing, Personal Care, Home Care, and Nutrition (Ice Cream demerged December 2025; Nutrition to be substantially disposed via the McCormick Foods transaction signed March 2026). The central return mechanism is a brand-equity and last-mile distribution moat — Dove, Hellmann's, Knorr, Axe/Lynx across 190+ countries — that has sustained operating margins in the 16.6–17.9% band through the FY2022 commodity spike and two years of portfolio restructuring. XBRL data through FY2025 shows ROIC recovering to 14.7% from a FY2024 trough of 11.5%, OCF stable at EUR 8.4bn, and goodwill declining EUR 4.6bn via impairment consistent with deliberate portfolio rationalisation. The central debate is whether the portfolio-simplification cycle will unlock a re-rating in ROIC and organic growth, or whether private-label penetration and EM currency devaluation erode the brand premium before restructuring benefits materialise. (§1.1)

5.6 Business Highlights — FY2025

Portfolio structure (as at FY2025 XBRL perimeter): - Beauty & Wellbeing — Dove, Lux, TRESemmé, Vaseline; est. ~24% of group revenue (segment revenue not tagged in ESEF) - Personal Care — Axe/Lynx, Rexona, Closeup; est. ~22% of group revenue - Home Care — Surf, Comfort, Domestos; est. ~19% of group revenue - Nutrition — Hellmann's, Knorr, Marmite (subject to McCormick transaction); est. ~22% of group revenue - Ice Cream — Magnum, Ben & Jerry's, Wall's; demerged December 2025; included in FY2025 XBRL for most of the year (~13%)

Ice Cream separation (December 2025): Magnum Ice Cream Co. demerger completed December 2025. Revenue perimeter will shrink approximately EUR 5–6bn in FY2026 (first full year post-demerger). The disposal gain is the primary driver of the FY2025 net income spike to EUR 9.5bn. Working capital release from the demerger explains part of the FY2025 balance sheet contraction (total assets EUR 79.8bn → EUR 70.5bn).

McCormick Foods transaction (signed March 2026): Hellmann's, Knorr, and Marmite (core Nutrition portfolio) agreed for sale at USD 44.8bn; expected close mid-2027. If closed, removes approximately EUR 10bn+ of Nutrition revenue from the FY2027+ perimeter. Regulatory clearance (EU DG COMP, US DOJ, UK CMA) is the principal risk. Exit condition 6 monitors collapse or repricing >20%.

EM organic growth: Approximately 60% of revenue from emerging markets (not tagged in ESEF; sourced from trading statements). India USG +7%, EM basket USG +5.7% as of Q1 2026 per config.yaml proxy note. Power Brand underlying volume growth +4.0% Q1 2026 — exit condition 7 threshold (< +1.5%) not triggered.

Capital returns: Financing CF EUR −6.8bn FY2025 (dividends ~EUR 4.0–4.5bn + buybacks ~EUR 1.5–2.5bn). Dividend yield approximately 3.71% at GBX 4,630 (config.yaml reference, 2026-07-16). McCormick/Ice Cream proceeds may fund accelerated buybacks in FY2026–FY2027.
