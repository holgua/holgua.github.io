PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

PayPal is a global two-sided digital payments network with 439 million active accounts, generating $33.2B in FY2025 revenue and $1.79T in total payment volume. The central return mechanism has been network-effect leverage combined with aggressive share buybacks: a 27% share-count reduction from FY2021's 1,168M to the 855M outstanding as of July 22, 2026 drove a 37% three-year EPS CAGR. The central debate is whether a new CEO (Enrique Lores, appointed February 2026) can re-accelerate branded checkout and execute the April 2026 strategic reorganisation (targeting $1.5B annualised gross savings) without impairing the margin discipline that drove FY2025 ROIC to 15.0%. Q2 2026 data confirms the investment-year thesis: TPV grew 10% but GAAP operating income fell 5% YoY, margin contracted to ~16.4%, and net income dropped 12%, as transaction expense growth outpaced revenue growth. EDGAR data through FY2025 confirms high earnings quality (Beneish M-Score −3.185, accruals ratios consistently negative) and a $14B net cash base, but the FY2026 reinvestment cycle introduces near-term EPS pressure.

PART VI — KEY SEGMENT AND PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

6.1 Total Payment Volume and Transaction Metrics

  • Q2 2026 TPV: ~$487.9B (+10% YoY vs $443.5B) — consistent acceleration from FY2025's +7% full-year rate; 10% growth maintained in H1 2026
  • Payment transactions Q2 2026: 6,750M (+8% YoY vs 6,226M) — transaction growth below TPV growth indicates continued mix shift toward higher-value Braintree transactions
  • Active accounts: 439M (flat YoY from Q2 2025 and unchanged from FY2025 year-end) — engagement increasing (transactions per account +3% to 60.0) while total user base is stable
  • Transactions per active account (trailing 12 months): 60.0 vs 58.3 — positive indicator of deepening platform engagement

6.2 Transaction Margin and Take Rate Dynamics

  • Transaction expense rate Q2 2026: 0.90% of TPV, up 1bp YoY — driven by higher Braintree mix (lower take-rate, higher processing cost) and unfavorable funding mix; partially offset by favorable merchant mix
  • Transaction and credit loss rate Q2 2026: 0.08% of TPV, down from 0.11% — significant improvement due to lower fraud losses and risk mitigation benefits
  • Revenue growth below TPV growth: Transaction revenue grew 5% while TPV grew 10% in Q2 2026, reflecting product mix changes, higher co-marketing campaigns with large merchants (recorded as revenue reductions), lower FX fee revenue, and lower partner incentives — take-rate compression in evidence

6.3 Braintree and Venmo

  • Braintree (Q2 2026): ~$400M incremental revenue contribution YoY — driven by TPV growth and transaction count; growth rate not separately disclosed but clearly the dominant driver of total revenue growth; transaction expense rate increase is partly Braintree's higher processing cost profile
  • Braintree (H1 2026): ~$810M incremental revenue YoY — by far the largest revenue growth driver for the period
  • Venmo (Q2 2026): ~$60M incremental revenue contribution YoY — driven by TPV and payment transaction growth
  • Venmo (H1 2026): ~$130M incremental revenue YoY — sustained positive momentum
  • PayPal branded (Q2 2026): ~$130M headwind from co-marketing campaigns with large merchants; also lower FX fee revenue — PayPal branded products contracted slightly on a net basis in Q2 2026, confirming the ongoing mix shift within the portfolio

6.4 Value-Added Services (OVAS)

  • Q2 2026 OVAS revenue: $850M, flat YoY ($847M in Q2 2025) — increases in loan fee income (+$40M) offset by lower interest on customer balances (−$40M) from lower prevailing interest rates
  • H1 2026 OVAS revenue: $1,702M (+5% YoY from $1,622M) — partner institution revenue +$100M (revenue share from PayPal/Venmo credit cards, above minimum threshold), loan fee income +$80M; partially offset by lower interest on customer balances (−$60M) and Honey/other decline (−$40M)
  • Credit quality (Q2 2026 vs Q2 2025): Consumer net charge-off rate 4.8% (up from 3.6%); merchant net charge-off rate 7.6% (up from 6.1%) — deterioration year-over-year, partially reflecting portfolio composition changes; consumer receivables 96.6% current (up from 96.2%); merchant receivables 89.6% current (down marginally from 89.9%); allowance for consumer loans $348M, relatively stable vs $369M at year-end
  • BNPL loan sales: $15.9B loans sold (unpaid balance) in H1 2026 via forward flow arrangements (vs $11.6B in H1 2025) — continued off-balance-sheet risk management; held-for-sale balance $1.9B at June 30, 2026

Appendix G — Glossary & Methodology Notes (Sentinel)

Glossary

Active Account: A PayPal-registered account that has completed at least one transaction in the past 12 months (excluding gateway-exclusive transactions). Used as primary scale metric.

BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later): PayPal's short-term instalment loan products offered at checkout. Available in US, UK, Germany, France, Australia, Japan (Paidy). Core consumer credit product.

Beneish M-Score: Statistical model using 8 financial ratio variables to assess probability of earnings manipulation. Threshold: > −1.78 signals elevated risk. FY2025 score: −3.185 (no manipulation signal).

Accruals Ratio: (Net Income − Operating Cash Flow) / Total Assets. Negative ratio indicates cash earnings quality is high (OCF > Net Income on an asset-normalised basis). FY2025: −0.015 (high quality).

FCF (Free Cash Flow): Operating Cash Flow minus Capital Expenditures, as computed from XBRL data. FY2025: $5,564M. H1 2026: ~$2,678M (OCF $3,117M − CapEx $439M).

End of Brief.

NOPAT: Net Operating Profit After Tax = Operating Income × (1 − Effective Tax Rate). Used in ROIC and DCF calculations.

ROIC: Return on Invested Capital = NOPAT / (Total Assets − Current Liabilities). FY2025: 15.0%.

TM$ / Transaction Margin Dollars: PayPal non-GAAP metric = Transaction Revenue + OVAS Revenue − Transaction Expense − Transaction and Credit Losses. The primary profitability driver management tracks. "TM$ ex-interest on customer balances" removes interest rate sensitivity.

TPV (Total Payment Volume): The value of payments, net of reversals, successfully completed on PayPal's platform (excluding gateway-only transactions). Q2 2026: ~$487.9B (+10% YoY). FY2025: $1.79T.

Transaction Expense Rate: Transaction expense divided by TPV. Q2 2026: 0.90% (up from 0.89% prior year). Driven by Braintree mix and funding mix.

Strategic Reorganisation (April 2026): CEO Lores's programme to realign operating structure, accelerate AI/automation adoption, target $1.5B gross annualised savings over 2–3 years, with $400M run-rate in Phase 1 expected by end of 2026.
