PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Berkshire Hathaway is a permanent-capital conglomerate whose core engine is the insurance float model: GEICO and Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance generate ~$176B in float invested at Berkshire's discretion, producing $12.5B in annual investment income that competitors cannot replicate. This float is layered onto wholly owned subsidiaries across railroads (BNSF $5.5B operating earnings), regulated utilities (BHE), and manufacturing/service/retail ($13.6B), delivering $38.4B in consolidated operating earnings for FY2025 — a 34% YoY gain. The central debate is post-Buffett transition risk: Gregory Abel assumed the CEO role on January 1, 2026, with a fortress balance sheet ($210B cash/T-bills, zero holding-company debt) but an untested acquisition track record at scale. EDGAR data confirm the structural advantages are durable — book value per Class B equivalent compounded ~13% annually over FY2023–2025, and the operating earnings engine is clearly intact.
