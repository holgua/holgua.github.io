PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Dynatrace, Inc. (NYSE: DT) is a Delaware-incorporated enterprise SaaS company providing an AI-powered observability and security platform built on three proprietary capabilities: Grail (causal data lakehouse), Smartscape (real-time topology mapping), and Dynatrace Intelligence (agentic AI). The central return mechanism is net expansion within a ~4,100-enterprise installed base — ARR growing 18% YoY to $2,054M with DBNRR of 110% — combined with systematic operating leverage driving GAAP EBIT margin from 8% (FY2023) to 12% (FY2026). The central debate is whether 14.8% SBC/revenue structurally destroys value for owners (owner FCF of only $230M vs reported $530M GAAP FCF) or whether ongoing buybacks and margin expansion will normalize the dilution. EDGAR evidence supports both strong cash generation and material equity cost, leaving the owner-FCF yield as the key valuation tension.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Moat type: Switching-cost + proprietary architecture (Grail causally connected data lakehouse).
  • Strongest evidence: DBNRR 110%; ARR ~102% of LTM subscription revenue; 7 consecutive years 18%+ growth.
  • Durability: Narrow-to-Wide; competitors (Datadog, Elastic) cannot replicate Smartscape topology mapping without full rearchitecture.
  • Trajectory: Stable-to-Widening; agentic AI (Dynatrace Intelligence) trained on 4,100 enterprise environments creates proprietary data moat.
  • R&D investment of 23.5% of revenue ($474.3M FY2026) sustains platform breadth lead over point-solution rivals.
