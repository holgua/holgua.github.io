5. Owner Perspective (Buffett Frame)

"Would Berkshire deploy cash into this basket today?"

On pure EY-vs-risk-free arithmetic, no: the basket yields 4.59% vs. 4.724% risk-free on the USD 10Y, meaning a passive Treasury position earns more than the equity basket on a trailing basis. Buffett historically holds cash when risk-free rates exceed the equity earnings yield — the arithmetic signal is negative. However, the IQQH thesis is explicitly thematic, not a yield-vs-risk-free trade: the position is underwritten by the global decarbonisation capex cycle, regulatory mandates (EU Green Deal, US IRA), and the structural cost advantage of renewables, none of which appear in trailing P/E. The underlying quality is adequate: a 14.31% median ROE confirms the core businesses (First Solar, Nextpower, Bloom Energy, Vestas) are compounding capital at rates well above cost of equity. A Buffett-style investor would not deploy new capital at 21.77× trailing P/E when 10Y Treasuries yield more, but the existing position is supported by the thematic thesis as long as the structural tailwinds remain intact and none of exit conditions 1–6 are triggered.
