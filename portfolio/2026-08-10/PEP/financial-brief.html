PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

PepsiCo is a wide-moat consumer staples compounder with durable pricing power across iconic global brands (Lay's, Pepsi, Gatorade, Quaker, Mountain Dew), generating $93.9B in revenue across six reportable segments with ~44% international exposure. The central return mechanism is pricing-led growth layered on a direct-store-delivery distribution network that is structurally difficult to replicate, with ROIC of 12.5–15.3% consistently above estimated WACC. The central debate is whether GLP-1 medications and private-label competition structurally impair convenient foods demand, and whether the 2.48x Net Debt/EBITDA leverage trajectory can reverse without sacrificing the 54-year dividend growth streak. EDGAR data through Q2 2026 supports the base-case recovery: organic volume grew at the highest rate since 2022, gross margins recovered to 55.2% in Q1 2026, and management reaffirmed guidance of +2–4% organic revenue and +4–6% Core CC EPS for full-year 2026.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Moat type: Wide — brand portfolio (Lay's #1 global snack), DSD distribution, and manufacturing scale (§9.1)
  • Brand evidence: 54% gross margin corridor sustained across 5 years through commodity cycles (§3.2)
  • Distribution moat: North American DSD system provides shelf placement and merchandising that new entrants cannot replicate at scale (§9.1)
  • ROIC anchor: 12.5–15.3% ROIC above estimated WACC of 6–8% across all three measured years (§6.1)
  • Trajectory: Stable — GLP-1 and private-label substitution are secular headwinds but have not compressed the gross margin corridor

PART IV — MANAGEMENT (DISTILLED)

4.1 Leadership, Capital Allocation, Insider Activity

  • CEO: Ramon L. Laguarta, Chairman & CEO since 2018; CFO: Steve Schmitt, appointed Nov 2025 (transition from James Caulfield) (§2.1)
  • Capital return cadence: $8.9B total shareholder returns guided for FY2026: $7.9B dividends + $1.0B buybacks; DPS CAGR 7.2% (FY2021–FY2025) (§4.4)
  • Dividend: 54 consecutive annual increases; 4th consecutive increase of 4% effective June 2026 (§4.4)
  • Insider direction: All Form 4 activity is compensation-related RSU/PSU vesting; no open-market purchases or accelerated discretionary sales detected (§2.3)
  • Governance: No restatements; single 2022 CORRESP letter on proxy disclosure, not accounting; KPMG auditor since 1990 (§2.4)
