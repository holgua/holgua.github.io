PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

OMV AG is Austria's largest integrated oil, gas and chemicals company, earning margin across E&P (Austria/CEE/Norway/UAE), Refining & Petrochemicals (three European refineries), and a 46.94% equity-method stake in Borouge Group International AG (BGI) — the world's 4th-largest polyolefin producer formed in March 2026. The narrow moat rests on ÖBAG's 31.5% state anchor protecting E&P licences and CEE retail network density in Austria and Romania, durable but non-self-reinforcing advantages. The central debate is whether the resilient OCF engine (>€5bn for five consecutive years despite a 61% revenue decline from the FY2022 commodity peak) can survive rising CapEx intensity, a structurally elevated ETR (51–58%), and EU ETS free-allocation phase-down, while the BGI chemicals pivot generates returns above WACC. XBRL data confirms strong cash-earnings quality (accruals ratio −0.08) but reveals critical data gaps (CapEx, net debt, FCF not tagged), leaving the dividend sustainability (86% payout ratio) as the key unresolved risk at a price near Bull Case fair value (~€56.90 vs. base ~€38).
