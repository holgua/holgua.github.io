Under Hood Lubrication and Maintenance · 53-01-01a

With battery temperature at 80 degrees F, the battery should have a specific gravity reading of not less than 1.250. If battery requires charging, refer to Figure 2 for battery charging rates.

Check Brake Master Cylinder Fluid Level

  1. Push the master cylinder cap retainer to one side and remove the filler cap from the master cylinder. The diaphragm which seals the master cylinder should come off with the cap.
  2. Fill the reservoir to 1/4 inch from the top
  3. Install the filler cap, making sure that the diaphragm is properly seated in the cap. Use only brake fluid meeting specifications.

Steering Gear Housing Lubricant Level Check—Manual Steering—Ford, Mercury and Meteor

Remove the filler plug and lower cover bolt from cover to expose holes. Slowly turn the steering wheel to the left stop; lubricant should rise within the lower cover bolt hole; then slowly turn the steering wheel to the right stop; lubricant should rise within the filler plug hole. If lubricant does not rise in both the cover bolt hole and the filler plug hole, add lubricant meeting specifications.

Steering Gear Housing Lubricant Level Check—Manual and Linkage Type Power Steering

Fairlane, Falcon and Montego

Remove filler plug and lower cover bolt from cover to expose holes. Slowly turn the steering wheel to the left stop; lubricant should rise within the lower cover bolt hole; then slowly turn the steering wheel to the right stop; lubricant should rise within the filler plug hole. If lubricant does not rise in both the cover bolt hole and the filler plug hole, add lubricant meeting specifications.

Cougar and Mustang

Remove filler plug and upper cover bolt from cover to expose holes. Slowly turn the steering wheel to the right stop; lubricant should rise with the upper cover bolt hole; then slowly turn the steering wheel to the left stop; lubricant should rise within the filler plug hole. If lubricant does not rise in both the cover bolt hole and the filler plug hole, add lubricant meeting specifications.

Check Power Steering Pump Reservoir Fluid Level

Run the engine until the fluid is at normal operating temperature. Then turn the steering wheel all the way to the left and right several times, and shut off the engine.

  1. Check the fluid level in the power steering reservoir. The level must show on cross hatching between the bottom of the dipstick and the full mark. Do not overfill. Remove excess fluid with a suction device.
  2. If the level is low, add enough automatic transmission fluid which meets specifications to raise the level to the F mark on the dipstick.
