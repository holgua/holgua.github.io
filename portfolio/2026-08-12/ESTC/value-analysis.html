§ 2. Valuation Method

Owner Earnings Yield (OEY) Method

The config.yaml specifies method: "owner_earnings_yield". The numerator is owner_earnings_override_usd: $321,800,000 (FY2026 audited raw FCF).

The SBC distinction — why raw FCF, not net-of-SBC FCF:

FY2026 SBC was $298.4M. Thesis-Validate.py's default behaviour deducts SBC from FCF before computing OEY, reducing the effective numerator to approximately $23.4M ($321.8M − $298.4M). This produces a spurious OEY of approximately 0.39% at $57.02 — a near-zero yield that generates a FAIR-VALUE-DRIFT warning in the ReValidation and signals a BUY at almost any price, which is analytically misleading.

The config explicitly overrides this with owner_earnings_override_usd: 321800000 because:
1. The buy/hold/trim thresholds (5.50% / 3.04% / 2.20%) were calibrated to the $321.8M raw FCF basis (Financial-Brief §5.1)
2. Raw FCF ($321.8M, 18.5% margin) is the cash actually generated by the business before non-cash equity compensation
3. The SBC-adjusted view ($23.4M) understates economic earnings for a software company where SBC is a structural feature of the compensation model, not a one-time charge
4. For comparability, any peer multiple applied to enterprise value implicitly includes SBC as an operating cost — raw FCF is the appropriate numerator when using an OEY band calibrated to absolute thresholds

- Market cap = $57.02 × 107.2M shares = $6,112.5M
- OEY = $321.8M / $6,112.5M = 5.27%
- Position vs. thresholds: Buy ≥5.50% | Fair value 3.04% | Trim ≤2.20%

§ 3. Moat Assessment

Switching Costs: Present and Strong

The primary moat source is switching costs from production-embedded pipelines. Once an organisation's log analytics, APM, SIEM, or search infrastructure is built on Elasticsearch, migration requires:
- Re-ingestion of all historical data into a new store
- Rebuilding alerting rules, dashboards, and detection logic
- Retraining operations and security teams
- Regression-testing production search relevance
- Potential downtime risk during migration

These are not trivial costs for organisations running Elasticsearch at petabyte scale across multiple use cases. The 112% NER confirms that customers are expanding rather than migrating away — the primary empirical evidence that switching costs are intact.

The 10-K corroborates this directly: "Our customers often significantly expand their usage of our products and services over time. Expansion includes increasing the number of developers and practitioners using our products, increasing the utilization of our products for a particular use case, and utilizing our products to address new use cases."

Intangible Assets: Elasticsearch Developer Brand + FedRAMP Gate

Elasticsearch developer brand: Elasticsearch is the most downloaded open source vector database (10-K, Item 1) and has been the dominant enterprise search engine for over a decade. This brand creates an asymmetric distribution channel — prospects often arrive already familiar with the technology, reducing Elastic's customer acquisition cost. No quantitative CAC is disclosed in the 10-K, but the self-service download model (free trial, community edition) creates a large funnel.

FedRAMP High regulatory gate: Achieving FedRAMP High authorization (Q4 FY2026) creates a regulatory moat for the federal market segment. Competitors without FedRAMP High certification are structurally ineligible for high-security federal contracts. This moat is durable (FedRAMP certifications are expensive and time-consuming to obtain and maintain) and defensible (competitors must independently achieve certification; Elastic's existing certification cannot be replicated by imitation).

Network Effects: None Claimed

The config.yaml thesis explicitly states: "no network effects or cost advantages are claimed." This is accurate — Elasticsearch is not a network-effect business. Each customer deployment is independent; the value of Elastic's platform does not scale with the number of other customers on the platform.

Cost Advantages: None Claimed

Elastic does not hold a structural cost advantage over competitors. R&D at 26.9% of revenue and S&M at 35.1% of revenue are typical for growth-stage enterprise software, not indicative of a cost leader.

Overall Moat Rating: Narrow

The switching cost moat is genuine and confirmed by NER, but it is narrow rather than wide because:
1. AWS OpenSearch provides a credible, free alternative that lowers the switching cost for self-managed workloads embedded in the AWS ecosystem
2. Datadog has successfully displaced Elastic in some observability accounts despite high switching costs — the moat is sticky but not impenetrable
3. Pure-play vector databases (Pinecone, Qdrant) are gaining traction for greenfield AI workloads where no historical switching cost exists

The FedRAMP intangible asset is a meaningful enhancement to the moat in the federal segment specifically, but insufficient to upgrade the overall rating to Wide in the absence of broader structural barriers.

§ 9. Risks and Mitigants

Permanent-Loss Risk (Bear Case, 20% Probability)

The bear DCF at $29.28 implies a -49% loss from $57.02. This is not a tail scenario — it is assigned 20% probability, meaning one in five outcomes. Bear triggers: AWS OpenSearch commoditises self-managed renewals faster than Elastic Cloud can absorb the attrition; NER falls below 105% for two consecutive quarters; FCF margin compresses to the 14% floor; Datadog widens the observability gap above 15pp for two fiscal years.

Mitigant: The thesis_broken action plan (config.yaml) is designed for rapid exit: "Exit the full position within 30 days of triggering any written exit condition." The discipline of pre-committed exit conditions limits permanent loss from a bear outcome if executed as written.

SBC Dilution at High Revenue Multiple

At 17.7% of revenue ($298.4M), SBC is elevated versus mature software peers. On a net-of-SBC FCF basis, the economic earning yield is approximately $23.4M / $6.1B market cap = 0.39% — not a meaningful return to shareholders. The raw FCF basis ($321.8M) is the correct anchor for comparing across the OEY band thresholds (which were calibrated to raw FCF), but investors should be aware that SBC dilution is ongoing and consumes approximately $298.4M / year of economic value before the buyback offsets it.

The buyback ($340M at $76.91) has partially offset SBC dilution by reducing share count (net: ~-2.95M shares in FY2026), but the cost was above the current market price — dilution management is directionally correct but cost-inefficient at current prices.

GAAP Profitability Timeline Risk (Exit Condition 6)

GAAP operating margin is -1.9% in FY2026. Exit condition (6): GAAP operating margin not positive by FY2028. At ~400bps/year improvement, FY2028 positive GAAP operating income is feasible. If margin improvement stalls (e.g., SBC re-accelerates, headcount grows ahead of revenue), the FY2028 target becomes at risk. This is currently classified TRACKING — on path, not yet achieved.

Senior Notes Maturity (July 2029)

$575M Senior Notes (4.125%) mature in July 2029. With ~$1.37B in liquid assets ($795M net cash), this is not a near-term liquidity risk. Refinancing at then-current interest rates could modestly increase interest expense. Not a primary value driver but relevant to the non-GAAP/GAAP bridge.

Non-Cash Tax Asset Quality

The $434.9M non-cash income tax benefit in FY2026 reflects a reassessment of deferred tax asset realizability (Netherlands, UK, California). If future business performance reverses, impairment of these deferred tax assets would generate a material non-cash charge, potentially creating GAAP net losses and causing headline noise — though it would not affect FCF. Investors focused on GAAP metrics should track deferred tax asset health in future 10-K filings.
