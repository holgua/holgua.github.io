PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Datadog is the category-defining AI-powered observability and security platform for cloud-native infrastructure, serving 33,400 customers across 16+ product modules via a land-and-expand model that converts low-CAC initial deployments into usage-driven revenue growth. The business generates exceptional free cash flow (~29% simple FCF margin in FY2025) rooted in deeply embedded instrumentation agents, proprietary cross-telemetry correlation, and growing AI workload monitoring demand. The central debate is whether 25%+ revenue growth can be sustained into FY2028 as the largest customer begins reducing usage in Q3 2026, NRR holds in the low-120% range rather than re-accelerating, and hyperscaler competition intensifies. EDGAR data shows accelerating top-line momentum (+36% YoY in Q2 2026), a strengthening balance sheet ($5.0B liquidity), and management guiding confidently to $4.45–4.47B FY2026 revenue — suggesting the bull case is tracking but the largest-customer headwind introduces near-term execution risk that demands monitoring.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Switching costs (primary): embedded agents across microservices/CI-CD represent months of re-instrumentation risk for any migration.
  • Network effect (secondary): unified data model across infra/APM/logs/security creates proprietary correlation intelligence at scale.
  • Intangibles: 6 consecutive Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader awards in APM; grassroots DevOps/SRE brand reduces CAC.
  • Moat width: Narrow-to-Wide — non-GAAP ROIC strongly positive; GAAP ROIC suppressed by $750.7M SBC in FY2025.
  • Trajectory: Widening — NRR low-120s; module cross-sell expanding; Bits suite and Adaptive ML position DDOG for AI-workload era.

PART IV — MANAGEMENT (DISTILLED)

4.1 Leadership, Capital Allocation, Insider Activity

  • CEO Olivier Pomel (co-founder, tenure ~16 yrs); CFO David Obstler (CFO since 2018, pre-IPO).
  • FY2025 CEO total comp ~$26.6M (base $450K + bonus $777K at 183% of target + RSUs/PSUs ~$25.1M).
  • Capital return cadence: no dividends, minimal buybacks; $5.0B held as cash and marketable securities.
  • Insider direction: net seller — CEO sold ~127K Class A Aug 2026 per 10b5-1 plan (dated Dec 15 2025); retains ~9.4M total shares.
  • Integrity flags: consistently conservative guidance (FY2025 beat by 8.2%); Nevada reincorporation Apr 2026 warrants monitoring.
