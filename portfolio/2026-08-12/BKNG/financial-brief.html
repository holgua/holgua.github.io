PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Booking Holdings is the world's leading online travel marketplace by room nights booked, operating five consumer-facing brands across 220+ countries on an asset-light model. The business generates exceptional free cash flow — $9.1B in FY2025 at a 33.8% FCF margin — underpinned by a two-sided network of 4.4M+ properties and ~1.24B annual room nights that creates durable switching-cost and scale advantages. ROIC accelerated to 45% in FY2025, FCF quality is exceptional (accruals ratio persistently below −0.23), and management returned over 100% of cumulative FCF to shareholders since 2022. The primary counter-evidence is the FY2025 KAYAK goodwill impairment ($180M) — an early AI disruption signal — and multi-jurisdiction EU regulatory risk (Spain CNMC, DMA gatekeeper designation) that could structurally reduce pricing power.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Network effects: 4.4M properties + Genius loyalty (levels 2/3 = high-50s% of room nights) reinforce two-sided flywheel
  • Switching costs: Property management tool integrations, payment infrastructure, 3-tier Genius status, Connected Trip ecosystem
  • Intangibles/brand: Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda — collective marketing spend $8.2B in FY2025 across 40+ languages
  • Scale: AI tools, multilingual support, and payment infrastructure (100+ methods) unreplicable at equivalent cost by smaller entrants
  • Trajectory — STABLE with uncertainty: FCF margin 23.0% → 33.8% (FY2021–FY2025); KAYAK impairment signals AI already eroding meta-search segment

PART IV — MANAGEMENT (DISTILLED)

4.1 Leadership, Capital Allocation, Insider Activity

  • CEO Glenn Fogel (tenure since 2017): long-tenured, transparent on adversity (KAYAK impairment handled directly); no overclaiming in MD&A tone
  • Capital return discipline: $6.4B buybacks + $1.2B dividends in FY2025; $21.8B remaining authorisation; >100% of FCF returned since 2022
  • Insider ownership: All named insiders combined <1% of 783M shares; no discretionary open-market purchases; 260 Form 4 filings reflect RSU/PSU vesting mechanics only
  • Guidance track record: Transformation Program savings guidance raised mid-year (delivered $550M vs. $400–450M target); FY2023–FY2024 revenue guidance consistently accurate
  • Board: 11 directors (10 independent); no staggered board, no dual-class shares, no poison pill; Deloitte clean opinion Feb 2026
