2.1 Revenue Growth Decomposition

Datadog's revenue compounded from $1.03B (FY2021) to $3.43B (FY2025), a 35% four-year CAGR, with Q1 FY2026 re-accelerating to 32% YoY — $1.006B on a quarterly basis (Financial-Brief §5.1, §5.5).

Volume vs. price: Growth is overwhelmingly volume-driven — more customers adopting more products at higher usage levels. The usage-based model means revenue follows cloud workload growth rather than annual price increases. Customer count grew from roughly 14,000 (FY2021 estimated) to 32,700 (FY2025) (Financial-Brief §1.1). Price increases are not the growth engine; wallet-share expansion within existing accounts is.

Organic vs. acquired: Growth is almost entirely organic. Datadog's M&A record consists of small bolt-on technology acquisitions; there is no evidence of revenue-accretive large M&A in the financial record. The land-and-expand model grows ARR without requiring acquisitions (Financial-Brief §4.1).

Conclusion: Earnings quality is exceptional. The company converts revenue to cash at one of the highest rates in enterprise software. GAAP earnings are artificially depressed by SBC; FCF is the correct earnings proxy and is growing at ~25% annually.

5.2 Growth-Adjusted Valuation (PEG Analysis)

PEG ratio: Forward P/E 92.4× ÷ consensus EPS growth rate. Using the Financial-Analysis base-case EPS CAGR implied by $0.39 trailing EPS growing to $0.55 base FY2028E over three years: CAGR = (0.55/0.39)^(1/3) - 1 ≈ 12%. PEG = 92.4 / 12 ≈ 7.7× — deeply above the 2.0× "growth premium" threshold.

Using a more generous bull-scenario EPS of $1.20 over three years: CAGR = (1.20/0.39)^(1/3) - 1 ≈ 45%. PEG = 92.4 / 45 ≈ 2.1× — barely at the "growth premium" threshold, and only achievable in the bull scenario.

P/FCF-to-FCF-growth rate (PFCFG): P/FCF ~89× ÷ FCF CAGR. Using base FCF growing from $1.0B to $1.9B over three years: CAGR ≈ 24%. PFCFG = 89 / 24 ≈ 3.7× — above the 2.0× threshold. Using bull FCF ($2.7B): CAGR ≈ 39%. PFCFG = 89 / 39 ≈ 2.3× — still above threshold.

Reverse PEG — required growth rate at current P/E for fair value: At a target PEG of 1.5× and current forward P/E of 92.4×, the implied EPS growth rate required = 92.4 / 1.5 ≈ 62% annually. No scenario in the Financial Analysis projects EPS growth of 62% annually over three years — confirming the stock is pricing in assumptions beyond even the bull case in conventional PEG terms.

FCF-based assessment: P/FCF of 89× requires FCF growth of ~59% annually to achieve a 1.5× PFCFG — also beyond any modelled scenario.

Conclusion: By PEG and PFCFG metrics, the current valuation requires either an extremely bullish base-case assumption or willingness to pay a premium well above the "growth compounder" fair range. The stock is priced for perfection.

  • Land-and-expand NRR is the single most important metric, full stop. At NRR 120%, the installed base grows 20% annually before a single new customer. At NRR 110%, that rate halves. Watch threshold is 115% — below that, the growth mathematics deteriorate materially (Financial-Brief §12.4). Strengthens thesis above 120%; challenges it below 115%.

  • FedRAMP High is a real option, not a distraction. Achieved May 2026, unlocking DoD and high-impact civilian agencies. Currently non-material (<10% revenue), but government contracts are high-NRR, high-switching-cost engagements once embedded. Strengthens the bull case if the federal ramp executes in FY2027–FY2028.

  • SBC at 22% of revenue is an unresolved governance tension. Insiders are selling systematically — Pomel filed a $32.7M sale in July 2026. GAAP earnings are structurally depressed. This does not impair the FCF thesis, but it means headline EPS numbers will continue to disappoint on a GAAP basis and SBC is a real dilution cost. Challenges the thesis modestly.

  • The hyperscaler bundling risk is existential, not cyclical. AWS, Azure, and GCP all have native observability products and the ability to bundle them into enterprise agreements at marginal cost. This is the kill-switch risk — if even 15% of customers reduce Datadog spend to deploy a hyperscaler native tool, NRR falls below 115% and the growth thesis breaks. This risk is structural and permanent, not temporary.

  • At $266, you are buying the bull scenario at certainty pricing. The base-case central fair-value estimate is $115, the bull-case estimate is $603. The current price of $266 sits roughly 130% above base and 56% below bull — the market is implying a 65–70% probability-weighted outcome closer to bull than base. That is an uncomfortable starting point for a new position.
