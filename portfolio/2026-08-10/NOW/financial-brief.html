PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

ServiceNow is the dominant enterprise workflow-automation and AI platform, generating 97% subscription revenue with a 98% renewal rate sustained for seven consecutive years — evidence of a wide moat rooted in deep workflow integration and high switching costs. The central return mechanism is negative-working-capital SaaS: upfront annual billing drives FCF structurally above GAAP net income (FCF/Net Income 2.62× in FY2025), compounding a 34.5% GAAP FCF margin against 20%+ subscription revenue growth. The central debate is whether AI upsell (NowAssist >$1B ACV in Q2 2026), the Armis/Moveworks/Veza acquisition stack, and management's Rule of 60 ambition justify a 49× P/Owner Earnings multiple — or whether $7.5B post-Armis net debt, 14.7% SBC/revenue, Q2 2026 GAAP gross-margin compression to 70.7%, and an active DOJ investigation compress the multiple before the thesis plays out. EDGAR data confirms exceptional cash earnings quality (accruals ratio −0.142) and strong interest coverage (67.56×), supporting HOLD for existing holders while new capital awaits a better entry.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Moat type: Switching costs + network effects. Single data model across all enterprise departments creates deep lock-in. (§2.1, §2.3)
  • Strongest evidence: 98% annual renewal rate for 7 consecutive years (FY2019–FY2025) across ~8,700 customers. (§2.4)
  • Patent base: >2,000 issued patents; multi-instance architecture with customer-dedicated database layer. (§2.3)
  • Durability: Wide. "Now on Now" dogfooding and deep enterprise relationship depth described as "institutional knowledge." (§2.3)
  • Trajectory: Stable → Widening. AI Control Tower + Armis/Veza extend moat into security/governance TAM. (§2.5)
