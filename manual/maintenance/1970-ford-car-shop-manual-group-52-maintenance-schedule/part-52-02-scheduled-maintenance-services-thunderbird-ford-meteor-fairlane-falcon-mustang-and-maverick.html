More frequent service intervals will be required if the vehicle is operated in extremely dusty areas or for extended periods of idling; trailer towing, or short runs which prevent the engine from reaching normal operating temperature.

The cooling system should be inspected each 12,000 miles or 12 months. If the coolant is dirty or dusty in appearance the system should be drained and flushed. The radiator cap should be cleaned and the system refilled with the specified solution of coolant and water.
