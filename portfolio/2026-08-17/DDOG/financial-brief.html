PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Datadog is the category-defining AI-powered observability and security platform for cloud-native infrastructure, serving 33,400 customers across 16+ product modules with a land-and-expand model that compresses customer acquisition cost into long-term usage-driven retention. The business generates exceptional free cash flow (FCF margin ~29% in FY2025) with a structural advantage rooted in deeply embedded instrumentation agents, proprietary cross-telemetry data correlation, and growing AI workload monitoring demand. The central investment debate is whether Datadog can sustain 25%+ revenue growth into FY2028 as its largest customer begins reducing usage in Q3 2026, NRR holds in the low-120% range rather than re-accelerating to mid-120s, and competition from hyperscalers intensifies. EDGAR data shows accelerating top-line momentum (+36% YoY in Q2 2026), a strengthening balance sheet ($5.0B cash + securities), and management guiding confidently to $4.45–4.47B in FY2026 revenue — suggesting the bull case is tracking but the largest-customer headwind introduces near-term execution risk that demands monitoring.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Switching costs (primary): Re-instrumenting thousands of microservices for a competitor represents months of engineering effort and risk
  • Soft network effects: Unified data model across infra, APM, logs, security, and AI observability compounds proprietary correlation intelligence per dataset added
  • Intangibles: 6 consecutive Gartner MQ Leader in APM/Observability; grassroots DevOps/SRE brand lowers CAC
  • Trajectory widening: Revenue CAGR 26.9% (3yr); NRR low-120s; Bits suite + Adaptive ML acquisition expand into AI-adjacent markets pre-competition
  • ROIC caveat: GAAP ROIC -0.7% (FY2025) is SBC-distorted; non-GAAP operating income $768M (22% margin) on minimal tangible capital implies strong economic franchise

PART IV — MANAGEMENT (DISTILLED)

4.1 Leadership, Capital Allocation, Insider Activity

  • CEO Olivier Pomel: Co-founder; FY2025 total comp ~$26.6M; retains ~618K Class A + ~8.79M Class B post-Aug 2026 transactions
  • Guidance accuracy: Consistently conservative — FY2025 beat by +8.2% ($3.43B vs. $3.17B initial guide); FY2024 beat by +11%; no downward revisions in 3-year lookback
  • Insider selling: CEO sold 127K Class A shares (Aug 2026) pursuant to 10b5-1 plan dated Dec 15, 2025 — routine, not opportunistic; equals ~1.3% of total retained holdings
  • Capital allocation: No dividends; no formal buyback program; $5.0B liquidity deployed selectively via bolt-on M&A ($191.5M in H1 2026); bonus tied to net new ARR (183% payout FY2025)
  • Governance risk: Dual-class (Class B: 10 votes/share), staggered board, Nevada reincorporation (Apr 2026) — management control is very strong; minority shareholder recourse is limited
