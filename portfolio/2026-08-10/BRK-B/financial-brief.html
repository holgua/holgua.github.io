PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Berkshire Hathaway is a permanent capital holding company combining a ~$176 billion insurance float engine (GEICO, BH Primary, BHRG) deployed at near-zero cost into an equity portfolio and diversified operating subsidiaries, with CEO Gregory Abel (63) taking over from Buffett effective January 1, 2026 — removing the principal succession overhang. Operating earnings of ~$44.4 billion in FY2025 (not the GAAP net income of $67.0 billion, which is distorted by mark-to-market investment gains) reflect the true earnings power of the float machine, BNSF ($5.5 billion after-tax), BHE ($4.0 billion), and manufacturing/service subsidiaries ($13.6 billion). The primary near-term headwinds are BNSF volume pressure from coal decline, BHE wildfire liability (PacifiCorp, declining accruals), and the question of whether Abel will deploy Berkshire's $52.6 billion cash position with the same discipline as Buffett.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Insurance float at $176bn; GEICO CR 84.7%; BHRG/BH Primary profitable 3 consecutive years — scale moat, stable
  • $333bn combined statutory surplus + AA+/A++ ratings; unmatched balance sheet fortress — financial strength moat, stable
  • BNSF: 32,500 route miles Western US; >$50bn estimated replacement cost; physically irreplicable — network moat, stable
  • BHE: rate-regulated utility territories; 32,400 MW capacity; $38bn cumulative renewable investment — regulatory moat, stable
  • Revenue 3yr CAGR 7.1% (XBRL); decentralized model retains subsidiary management for decades — brand/talent moat, stable

PART IV — MANAGEMENT (DISTILLED)

4.1 Leadership, Capital Allocation, Insider Activity

  • Abel (CEO since Jan 2026): 33 yrs at BHE, $22M cash comp, no stock awards — aligned owner-operator pay model
  • Buffett (non-exec Chairman, ~30% voting power, 95): zero open-market sales; all Form 4 activity is charitable A→B conversions
  • Hamburg (CFO, 34 yrs): expected to retire; Abel to name successor — key transition watch item
  • Jain (Vice Chairman Insurance, 39 yrs): no identified successor; key-person risk in reinsurance pricing
  • Board: 62% independent; no staggered board; no poison pill; $100K–$22M pay range reflects genuine owner-operator ethos
