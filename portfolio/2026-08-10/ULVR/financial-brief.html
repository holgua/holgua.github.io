PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Unilever PLC is a global FMCG company with ~EUR 50.5bn in annual revenue across Beauty & Wellbeing, Personal Care, Home Care, and Nutrition (Ice Cream demerged December 2025). The central return mechanism is a brand-equity and last-mile distribution moat that has sustained operating margins in a 16.6–17.9% band through extreme commodity cycles and a decade of portfolio churn. The central debate is whether the ongoing portfolio-simplification cycle (Ice Cream demerger; McCormick Foods Nutrition disposal signed March 2026 for USD 44.8bn) will unlock a re-rating in ROIC and organic growth, or whether private-label penetration and EM currency devaluation will erode the brand premium before restructuring benefits materialise. XBRL data through FY2025 shows ROIC recovering to 14.7% from a FY2024 trough, OCF stable at EUR 8.4bn, and goodwill declining EUR 4.6bn via impairment — consistent with deliberate portfolio rationalisation, not distress.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Moat type: dual-source — brand equity (Dove, Hellmann's, Magnum, Knorr, Axe) + proprietary last-mile distribution in 190+ countries (§3.3)
  • Moat width: Wide — ROIC 14.7% vs. estimated EUR WACC 8–9%, spread of 5–7pp over 5-year XBRL horizon (§3.3)
  • Strongest evidence: operating margin held 16.6–17.9% through EUR 10bn commodity spike (FY2022) and portfolio restructuring (§3.3 + §5.1)
  • Trajectory: Stable-to-improving — ROIC recovered from FY2024 trough 11.5% to 14.7% FY2025; margin at 5-year high (§3.3)
  • Key threat: private-label penetration in DM foods (rising) and DTC beauty brands at the premium/niche end (§3.4)
