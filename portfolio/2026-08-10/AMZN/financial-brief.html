PART I — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.1 One-Paragraph Thesis

Amazon is a structurally advantaged multi-segment enterprise whose three mutually reinforcing pillars — AWS cloud infrastructure, high-frequency retail commerce, and digital advertising — generate increasingly durable economics at a scale that is extremely difficult to replicate. AWS is the central value engine: growing 20% in FY2025 and 28% in Q1 2026, it converts the secular AI/cloud shift into rapid margin expansion (AWS segment operating income $45.6B, ~35% segment margin), while Stores and Advertising generate powerful cash flows funding Amazon's capital intensity cycle. The central investment debate is whether Amazon's massive capex acceleration ($128.3B FY2025, nearly double FY2024) for AI infrastructure creates sustainable moats or destroys near-term FCF. Management-defined FCF fell to $11.2B (FY2025) despite XBRL narrow FCF of $132.8B, underscoring the investment cycle's depth. The thesis turns on AWS margin durability and custom-silicon differentiation, for which Q1 2026 evidence (28% AWS growth, Trainium/Graviton adoption) is encouraging but not conclusive.

PART III — COMPETITIVE POSITION (DISTILLED)

3.3 Moat — Type, Evidence, Trajectory

  • Cost/scale (AWS): Graviton custom silicon claims 60% energy savings; Trainium growing triple digits; 500+ services deepen switching costs. [WIDE]
  • Network effects (Marketplace): 3P seller flywheel → more selection → more buyers → more advertising revenue; data compounds at scale.
  • Intangibles (Prime/brand): 13B+ same/next day deliveries FY2025; Prime habit-driven loyalty creates high consumer switching costs.
  • Efficient scale (logistics): Proprietary last-mile network (fulfilment centres, delivery stations, Amazon Air) is capital-intensive and very difficult to replicate quickly.
  • Trajectory: Widening (conditional) — AWS margin expansion ($45.6B segment OI FY2025) and custom silicon widen the moat; conditional on CapEx returns materialising by FY2028.

8.2 Competitive & Technology Risks

  • AWS market share erosion (Azure/GCP): 2pp share loss at FY2025 base = ~$8.6B revenue headwind; simultaneous margin compression would reduce op. income ~$2.6B.
